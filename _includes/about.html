Hi! I'm Tejal. (she/her)

what I do

I'm a data and graphics reporter at NBC Local. Previously, I was a newsroom product manager at The Baltimore Banner. I studied data journalism at the Lede Program at Columbia University.

I served on the judging panel for the 2024 IRE Awards and presented The Banner's voter guide for the 2024 Maryland primary elections at NICAR. My work at The Banner earned us the Local Media Association Award for Best Local News Website in 2024 and the MDDC Award for General Website Excellence in 2024 and 2023. My team also supported the data pipeline for a Pulitzer Prize-winning investigative series on Baltimore's overdose crisis with The New York Times.

I'm always open to collaborate! Please get in touch.

how I got here

I researched news visualization and got involved in design for social impact at MIT (here's an interview on my experience). I majored in computer science at BITS Pilani in India, where I served as an editor for the university magazine.

Before I got into journalism, I built trading platforms at Goldman Sachs, where I was a finalist in their 2020 COVID-19 Visualization Datathon. I found my interest shifting towards using data to empower storytelling—placing me at the intersection of journalism, design, and technology.
