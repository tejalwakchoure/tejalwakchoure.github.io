The Census Bureau recently released annual US population changes on the state, county, and city levels for 2024.


scroll to explore

The rate of international migration into the state increased as well, following an upward trend since 2020 with a record 56,155 international migrants in 2024. Nearly 30% of this population moved into Fairfax County, offsetting its negative rate of domestic migration. That number might change under the Trump administration as law enforcement cracks down on immigration in the state.

Methodology

Population and geographic data was collected from the Census Bureau and analyzed in Excel. Map interactive was built using Mapbox Studio, Mapbox GL JS, and Scrollama.js. The line chart was built in Datawrapper.
