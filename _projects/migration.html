The Census Bureau recently released annual US population changes on the state, county, and city levels for 2024.


{% include migration-map.html %}

The rate of international migration into the state rose as well, following an upward trend since 2020 with a record +56,155 international migrants. Nearly 30 percent of this population moved into Fairfax County, offsetting its negative rate of domestic migration for a net positive rate of migration. That number might change under the Trump administration as law enforcement cracks down on immigration in the state.
